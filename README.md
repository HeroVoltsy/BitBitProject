# Sali Ka 🦜

**Your neighborhood, always up to something.**

👉 **Try the preview:** [mock-sali-ka.vercel.app](https://mock-sali-ka.vercel.app/)
*(hosted from a separate repo — see [Project status](#project-status) below)*

Sali Ka is a website for discovering and joining community events near you — from
Sunday markets and fun runs to concerts, clean-up drives, and pop-up bazaars.
Find something to do this weekend, host your own event, and check people in with
a simple QR code.

The name comes from the Filipino phrase *"Sali ka?"* — "Want to join?" — because
that's what the app is all about: making it easy to say yes to what's happening
around you.

Built by **BitBit** in Naga City, Camarines Sur. 🇵🇭

---

## What you can do

### 🔎 Find events
- Browse **events around you** on the Home page, with pictures, dates, and how many
  people are going.
- **Filter** by category (Market, Music, Sports, Volunteer, Fun Run, Concert,
  Pop-Up), by location, or by date.
- Use the **Schedule** to see a calendar of what's happening each day, and jump to
  any month or year.
- Use **Explore** to see events on a map.

### ❤️ Save the ones you like
- Tap the **heart** on any event to save it.
- Find all your saved events later under **Profile → Liked**.

### 🙋 Join events
- Tap **Join This Event** to say you're going.
- Show your personal **QR code** at the event to check in.
- See every event you're attending under **Profile → Attendee**.

### 📣 Host your own
- Post an event in a few steps — add a title, date, time, place, category, and
  description.
- Use the **Check-in Scanner** to welcome attendees by scanning their QR codes.
- Keep track of who has arrived with the **Attendance List**.
- See your hosting history, ratings, and followers on your **Profile**.

### 👥 Personal and Business accounts
- Sign up as an individual, or as a **business** (like a café or shop) that hosts
  events for the community.

### 🌙 Comfortable to use
- Works on computers, tablets, and phones.
- Has a **Dark Mode** for easier reading at night.

## Safety, privacy, and community rules

We want Sali Ka to be a safe and welcoming place. Inside the app, under
**Settings → Support**, you'll find:

- **Terms & Conditions** — the rules for using Sali Ka.
- **Privacy Policy** — what information we collect and how we protect it, in line
  with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.
- **Community By-laws** — how members and hosts are expected to behave, and what
  happens when someone breaks the rules.

## Project status

Sali Ka is **still being built**. 🚧

You can try a **working preview** at
**[mock-sali-ka.vercel.app](https://mock-sali-ka.vercel.app/)**. You can click
through every page and try every feature, but the events and people you see are
**sample data**, and nothing you do is saved permanently yet. Real accounts and
saved data are coming as we finish connecting the website to our server.

> **Note:** The preview is published from a separate repository,
> [gbzor/mock-sali-ka](https://github.com/gbzor/mock-sali-ka), which holds a copy
> of this project's website design. We copy updates from here to there, so the
> preview may sometimes be a step behind what's in this repo.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an **Issue** here on GitHub and tell us about it.
